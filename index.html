Bronze · Singapore RailTech Grand Challenge 2024 (IHL)

TrackSafe

Real-time LoRa tracking of maintenance crews on MRT tracks.

Every crew member wears a helmet fitted with an ESP32 LoRa transmitter that broadcasts its helmet ID and battery level every 6 seconds. Receivers along the work zone pick up each packet, read its RSSI and SNR, and relay it over Wi-Fi through the cloud to the dashboard at the Operations Control Centre (OCC). With several people on the same stretch of track, the OCC sees every helmet live, and an out-of-bounds alert fires the moment a helmet is picked up by a receiver outside its assigned zone. I led the hardware design and the multi-node network architecture.

Featured · Interclean Amsterdam & Engineering Tech Show

META-HUB Knowledge Platform

An AI-powered resource portal for Metabots Singapore.

META-HUB is the resource portal for Metabots' range of cleaning and service robots. Staff and clients browse products and solutions, search every document from one bar, or ask the built-in AI chatbot. Admins manage users and assets and can switch to "View as User" to check what clients see. I built the backend and set up the AWS services from scratch: Bedrock and Lex power the chatbot, Pinecone handles retrieval, and Lambda, EC2, RDS and Glue run the services and data pipeline. It replaced scattered, outdated document versions with one secure source of truth.

Silver & Best Prototype · IdeateCOMM Challenge 2025

GearCharge · Ageless Bicycle Singapore

BLE + RFID bike tracking that alerts volunteers before a bike goes missing.

Ageless Bicyclists runs community rides for seniors and needed to keep track of every bike inside an event zone. Each bike carries a BLE transmitter, and riders log in and out with an RFID tag. The receiver station, built on an ESP32-WROOM-32E, captures every bike's Bluetooth signal and uses its RSSI to tell whether the bike is still in the zone. Over Wi-Fi it links to the Blynk IoT app, which updates volunteers' phones when a rider takes a bike out of the zone or a bike is moved without a login. One station serves many bikes, with no SIM card and no data plan.

Smart Trolley

Lee Kuan Yew Technology Award

Take Me Home · Smart Trolley

Each trolley hides a TTGO LoRa board with a GPS module. When it leaves the supermarket's geofence, a buzzer prompts the shopper to return it, and its coordinates are sent over LoRa to the retailer and logged in Google Sheets automatically. One click on a row opens the exact spot in Google Maps, and the growing dataset feeds a linear-regression model that predicts where trolleys get abandoned. No SIM card or data plan needed.

  • GPS

Silver · The Sixth Sense Competition 2022 (SICK)

COVID-19 Digital Lanyard

A hands-free lanyard that keeps safe distancing without a supervisor. Its ESP32-CAM uses on-board AI to measure the distance to the person in front and flashes its LED when two people are closer than 1 m. An Omron D6T thermal sensor reads skin temperature and vibrates above 34 °C, and photos of close contacts go to Google Photos, timestamped for contact tracing.

U7
Gravity Storage

1st Runner-Up · SG Energy Grand Challenge 2023

Team Gravity · Smart Grid Storage

Surplus solar and wind power drives a motor that lifts heavy weights, storing the energy as gravitational potential energy. At peak demand the weights descend and turn the motor as a generator, giving 2–18 hours of clean storage at over 80% round-trip efficiency. A blockchain layer records production, distribution and consumption straight from smart meters, so power can be routed automatically between plants, homes and factories.
