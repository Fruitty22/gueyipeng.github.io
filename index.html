Bronze · Singapore RailTech Grand Challenge 2024 (IHL)

TrackSafe

Real-time LoRa tracking of maintenance crews on MRT tracks.

Night maintenance crews work on live track sections where the Operations Control Centre (OCC) needs to know exactly who is where. I led the design of helmet-mounted ESP32 + LoRa transmitters that send a helmet ID and battery level every 6 seconds to receivers along the work zone. The receivers read RSSI and SNR to work out which zone each person is in, raise out-of-bounds alerts, and forward everything over Wi-Fi to Firestore and a Flutter dashboard at the OCC.

  • Range1–3 km
  • Battery20–40 h
  • Unit costS$30 (S$10 at volume)
  • Update rateevery 6 s
  • ESP32
  • LoRaWAN
  • RSSI / SNR
  • Firestore
  • Flutter
  • 3D printing

Silver & Best Prototype · IdeateCOMM Challenge 2025

GearCharge · Ageless Bicycle Singapore

BLE + RFID bike tracking that alerts volunteers before a bike goes missing.

Ageless Bicyclists runs community rides for seniors and needed a way to keep track of bikes inside an event zone. Each bike carries a BLE transmitter with RFID login and logout. A station receives the BLE signals, uses RSSI to tell whether a bike is in the zone, and pushes Blynk notifications when a bike leaves the area or is moved without a login. No SIM card, no data plan.

  • Cost per bikeunder S$20
  • Topologymany bikes → one station
  • NextLoRa, solar station
  • BLE
  • RFID
  • RSSI
  • Wi-Fi
  • Blynk IoT
